# Governance

## Maintainer

The project maintainer stewards the roadmap, repository settings, security
response, releases, and final merge decisions. The maintainer may also perform
release-manager and reviewer duties while the project has a small maintainer
group.

## Decisions and contributions

Users may report problems, test releases, and share feedback. Contributors may
propose documentation, code, tests, and design changes through issues and pull
requests. Pull requests to `main` are the record of a change's rationale,
validation, risks, and release notes.

The maintainer makes final decisions with attention to project scope, user
impact, security, maintenance cost, and the documented contribution policy.
For conduct concerns, use the contact details in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
