# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, or
background.

## Expected behavior

Participate constructively, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and
respect differing viewpoints. Do not engage in harassment, discrimination,
threats, personal attacks, or the disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's
[security contact](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
with appropriate discretion. The maintainer may request changes, remove
content, or restrict participation to protect the community.
